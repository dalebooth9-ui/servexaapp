- Sticker-only logo substitution lives in riserStickers and retains the original paperwork logo — why: every print entry shares identical geometry without changing report branding.
- Assets sync outlet suffixes through sync_riser_outlet_codes with an asset-row lock and persistent high-water mark; consumers use fitted saved numbers rather than count ranges, and stickerSlots starts each selected riser on a new row — why: removed numbers are never reused and new higher suffixes remain visible and scannable.
