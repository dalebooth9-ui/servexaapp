- Printed Customer/Site Details resolve through `siteDetailLines`/`siteDetailsText` in `src/lib/siteDetailsPrint.ts` (customer, then site name, then site address; lines that repeat something already printed are dropped and the postcode prints once) — why: paper sheets, Word documents and PDF headers must print the same identity wording rather than each deduplicating it differently.
- Asset duplicate merges require a server preview token, retain canonical UUID/tag aliases and collision snapshots; writers reuse `upsert_asset_rows` — why: no automatic deletion, lost history or broken printed codes.
- QR reprints reuse existingRiserLabels and buildStickerPackPdf; only log_riser_label_reprints records validated office requests — why: replacement printing cannot change fitted QR identities or fabricate audit entries.
- Sticker logos resolve through resolveStickerBranding in src/lib/stickerBranding.ts (customer logo only when use_print_branding is on, plain with a note when that customer has no logo, else company logo); the Viva sticker crop lives in riserStickers — why: stickers follow the same opt-in as reports, per riser site, without changing geometry.
- Assets sync outlet suffixes through sync_riser_outlet_codes with an asset-row lock and persistent high-water mark; consumers use fitted saved numbers rather than count ranges, and stickerSlots starts each selected riser on a new row — why: removed numbers are never reused and new higher suffixes remain visible and scannable.
- Defects store scheduled_job_id/visit_id, scheduled_asset_id and works/materials; office attachment uses attach_defects_to_job and outcomes use record_carried_remedial. All PDF paths read job_carried_remedials and propagate errors — why: customer/org validation preserves source history and general items cannot silently disappear.
- Quote price resolution uses customerCatalogue/priceSuggestions server-side; price_needed flags unpriced rows, servicing stays separate, and refresh_quote_charges respects persisted auto/override/removed modes — why: models cannot invent prices or re-add a waived charge.
